About Me

A little more about me

Hi! I'm so happy to see you here. Welcome! As long as I can remember, I've always loved capturing moments: friends, family, things, and places.

I studied Fashion Design and graduated with honors at IBERO in Mexico City, where I took photography and editing classes. That experience gave me a strong technical foundation, which I later used for different projects and eventually for my own business.

Becoming a mom has transformed me in every possible way. Now I know that moments are fleeting, and that photographs are a beautiful gift for today - but an incredible treasure for the future.
